# Reuse Terms & Provenance

This document records the provenance and reuse terms for the team's published data assets.

## Derived assets

### Product Sentiment Dataset

- **Provenance**: derived from upstream_review_corpus and upstream_social_feed.
- **Reuse terms**: **Non-commercial**. This asset is released under the **CC BY-NC 4.0** license. Commercial use is **not permitted** because one of its upstream sources (upstream_social_feed) is licensed under a non-commercial license. Non-commercial use with attribution is allowed.

### Financial News Summaries

- **Provenance**: derived from upstream_news_articles and upstream_review_corpus.
- **Reuse terms**: **Commercial use is permitted with attribution**. This asset is released under the **CC BY 4.0** license. All upstream sources permit commercial reuse, so this derived asset may be used commercially provided attribution is given.
